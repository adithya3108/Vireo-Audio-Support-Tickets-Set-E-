B. Operational cross-check: 1,118 tickets an agent handed to another team

When an agent moves a ticket to another team, that is a human judgement of who owns it, made independently of this tool. Share of those hand-offs where the destination team owns the category:

Bot tag: 0.0% (by construction low: these are the tickets the bot routed elsewhere)

Our classifier: 58.6%

The classifier agrees with the agents' own re-routing decisions far more often than the bot tag does. Misses here are partly agents passing tickets on for other reasons (e.g. shift cover).
